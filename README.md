# 1122 Living Estates

Responsive coming-soon website for **1122 Living Estates**, a North Carolina family care home. Intended domain: **1122livingestates.com**.

## Preview locally

Open `index.html` in a browser, or run from the repository folder:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 1122 --bind 127.0.0.1
```

- Full website: http://127.0.0.1:1122/
- Framed phone preview: http://127.0.0.1:1122/mobile.html

No installation or build step is required. All images, styles, and scripts are local.

## Edit the site

- `index.html`: branding, content, care services, FAQs, and interest form.
- `styles.css`: colors, layout, typography, and mobile styles.
- `script.js`: mobile navigation and demonstration form behavior.
- `mobile.html`: interactive phone-frame preview.
- `assets/`: images used by the website.

## Form behavior

Inquiries are sent through FormSubmit to info@1122livingestates.com. The recipient must activate FormSubmit through the verification email before delivery works. The form validates required fields, prevents duplicate submissions, keeps entered details if delivery cannot be confirmed, and provides a direct email fallback. FormSubmit processes the submitted information and documents a 30-day submission archive. Do not collect medical records or sensitive health information through this general inquiry form.

## Content and launch details

The care copy reflects the owner's stated offerings, including personal care, cognitive support, aging in place and end-of-life support, an RN available as needed, and respite care. Hospice and other medical services are coordinated with outside providers. The site retains coming-soon status and does not invent an opening date, street address, or facility license claim.

Domain registration and custom-domain connection are not configured. There are no automatic publishing workflows. A separate Sites deployment is being prepared for review. The page requests that search engines do not index it.
