CARE & SUPPORT

Everyday support.
A personal touch.

Personal care, nursing support, and meaningful connection — brought together in the warmth of a small family care home.

A small home. Thoughtful, individual care.

In North Carolina, a family care home serves two to six residents. At 1122, our setting for no more than six residents pairs the comforts of home with personal care and supervision shaped around each person’s needs.

01 / PERSONAL CARE

Help with daily living

Respectful assistance with bathing, dressing, grooming, toileting, and everyday mobility, guided by an individual care plan.

02 / DAILY OVERSIGHT

24-hour support & supervision

Around-the-clock care presence, with supervision and assistance responsive to each resident’s assessed needs.

03 / MEDICATION SUPPORT

Help with medication routines

Assistance with prescribed medication routines in accordance with provider orders, care plans, and applicable staff training requirements.

04 / MEALS & NUTRITION

Nourishment throughout the day

Daily meals, encouragement with hydration, and mealtime assistance based on individual needs and dietary directions.

05 / A COMFORTABLE HOME

Housekeeping & laundry

Help with the everyday upkeep of living spaces, clothing, and linens, supporting a comfortable residential environment.

06 / COMPANIONSHIP

Connection & meaningful activities

Conversation, shared interests, and opportunities for engagement that respect each person’s preferences and pace.

07 / COGNITIVE SUPPORT

Dementia & Alzheimer’s support

Patient guidance, familiar routines, and reassuring companionship for residents living with dementia or Alzheimer’s. We get to know each person and tailor support to their individual needs.

08 / FAMILY & CARE PARTNERS

Communication & coordination

Working with families and outside healthcare providers to support care planning, appointments, and changing needs.

09 / AGING IN PLACE

Aging in place & end-of-life care

Comfort, familiarity, and dignity through life’s changing seasons. We support aging in place and compassionate end-of-life care, working closely with families, hospice, and outside healthcare providers to honor each resident’s needs and wishes.

10 / NURSING SUPPORT

RN available as needed

An RN is available as needed for assessment, consultation, and coordination with each resident’s healthcare providers. Nursing support brings added guidance as care needs change.

11 / SHORT-TERM STAYS

Respite care

A welcoming short-term stay with personal care, companionship, and the comforts of home. Respite care gives family caregivers time to rest, travel, or recharge, with the reassurance that their loved one has thoughtful support.

Every stay begins with a personal conversation and an assessment, so care reflects the individual. RN support is available as needed, and hospice and other medical services are coordinated with outside providers.

A FEW HELPFUL DETAILS

Getting to know 1122.

When will 1122 Living Estates open?

An opening date has not been announced. More details will be shared as plans are finalized.

What care services will be available?

Our services include assistance with daily living, 24-hour support and supervision, medication support, meals, housekeeping and laundry, companionship, dementia and Alzheimer’s support, respite care, and an RN available as needed. We also support aging in place and end-of-life care in coordination with hospice and outside providers. Each resident’s care begins with an individual assessment.

Can my loved one age in place at 1122?

Aging in place is central to our approach. As needs change, we work with residents, families, our RN, and outside providers to adapt support. End-of-life care includes compassionate coordination with hospice, with comfort and dignity at the center of every conversation.

Do you offer respite stays and RN support?

Yes. Respite care offers a supportive short-term stay, and an RN is available as needed for assessment, consultation, and care coordination. Contact us to discuss your family’s needs and stay availability.

What is a family care home?

In North Carolina, a family care home is an adult care home for two to six residents. 1122 offers this intimate residential approach, with no more than six residents and personal care and supervision tailored to individual needs.

Can I arrange a visit?

We look forward to welcoming families for a closer look. Get in touch to express your interest and receive tour updates as we prepare to open.
